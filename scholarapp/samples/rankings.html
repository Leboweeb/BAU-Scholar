About Beirut Arab University

Located in the Lebanese capital city of Beirut, Beirut Arab University is home to over 16,000 students and is one of the top universities in the Arab region. As well as its urban campus in Beirut, the university also has a suburban campus in Debbieh and another campus in Tripoli. Teaching at the university spans 10 different faculties, including engineering, medicine, science, and business administration. All programs are taught in English apart from some degrees offered by the Faculties of Arts and Law. More information about the English language admissions requirements can be found on the university website.
